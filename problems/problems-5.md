### problem set 5 (landscape of normal modal logics)

1. Use the fact that $\textsf{K}$ is sound and complete with respect to all frames to 
   justify the fact that $\Box \varphi/\varphi$ is a derived rule of inference for $\textsf{K}$. That is, if $\vdash_\textsf{K} \Box \varphi$,
   then $\vdash_\textsf{K} \varphi$.

      
2. Prove $\textsf{KB4}$ is a *proper* extension of $\textsf{K5}$. That is, 

   1. All theorems of $\textsf{K5}$ are theorems of $\textsf{KB4}$.

   2. Not all theorems of $\textsf{KB4}$ are theorems of $\textsf{K5}$.

      

3. Prove that $\textsf{K5}$ proves the *necessitation* of every axiom of $\textsf{KB4}$. That is:

   1. $\vdash_{\textsf{K5}} \Box (\varphi \to \Box \Diamond \varphi)$.

   2. $\vdash_{\textsf{K5}} \Box (\Box \varphi \to \Box \Box \varphi)$.

   

   
   *One option is to outline proof schemas for $\textsf{B}$ and $\textsf{4}$ respectively, but another option is to exploit 
   the fact that of $\textsf{K5}$ is sound and complete with respect to the class of euclidean models in order to give a semantic argument.*


4. Justify the facts below:

   1. $\Box \varphi / \varphi$ is *not* a derived rule of inference for $\textsf{K5}$.

   2. $\Box \varphi/\varphi$ is *not* a derived rule of inference for $\textsf{KB4}$.
